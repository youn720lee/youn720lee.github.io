Biography

I am a Staff Engineer in the AI Inspection Technology Group at Samsung Electronics (Global Technology Research), where my work centers on Vision Foundation Models (VFM) for manufacturing visual inspection. This spans foundation-model-based inspection platforms, anomaly and defect detection under limited data, and the data and model infrastructure that brings these models to production lines.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) in 2022, advised by Prof. Moongu Jeon. My doctoral research focused on robust visual perception in unconstrained environments, where recognition is threatened by bad weather, noise, occlusion, and dataset bias.

Industry Projects

R&D programmes I have worked on at Samsung Electronics, from vision inspection foundation models to the MLOps and robotics that put them on production lines.

Vision Foundation Model for AI Inspection

Apr 2026 – Dec 2026

A manufacturing-inspection foundation model that unifies external and in-house AI through grounding, trained on large-scale internal data.

  • Co-training of the VFM with LLM/VLM components for agentic inspection workflows.
  • Selectable inference modes — VFM only, instruction-conditioned, and post-hoc explanation.
  • Inspection data curated into reusable AI assets for domain specificity.
Vision Foundation ModelGroundingVLMAgentic AI

Re-inspection Automation Linked to AI Inspection Results

Feb 2026 – Nov 2026

Robot-based re-inspection that imitates how inspectors manipulate a product and change viewing conditions to confirm suspected defects.

  • Target observation poses and robot motions generated from AI inspection results.
  • Pairwise-ranking model that prioritises viewing angles and positions per defect type.
  • Multi-DOF hardware module for product manipulation under variable lighting and viewpoints.
RoboticsActive VisionPairwise RankingSmart Factory

DX AI Vision Platform Advancement

Dec 2025 – Oct 2026

Expanding the AI vision inspection platform into the DX-division standard platform with cloud infrastructure, data analytics, and AI agents.

  • Cloud-based infrastructure for large-scale inspection data and data-driven decision support.
  • AI agent that makes the VFM usable by field engineers (Data Agent — group Annual Award, 2026).
  • Extension to external sites such as package-damage inspection at logistics centres, with per-site MLOps.
MLOpsCloudAI AgentData Platform

Cloud-based Inspection Quality AI Service

Dec 2024 – Dec 2025

An MLOps service for deploying, operating, and maintaining deep-learning vision inspection in the field on Samsung SDS cloud services.

  • Cloud-native AI service — load balancer, MLOps platform, and Kubernetes engine.
  • Vision–text foundation model and generative AI for inspection.
  • Few-shot customisation of inspection models from small data (industry–academia collaboration).

MLOps AI Technology for Intelligent Inspection

Mar 2023 – Jan 2024

A systematic MLOps-based solution for keeping DX product-inspection AI models performant with minimal manual effort.

  • Data cleansing and automatic discovery of new defect types (−25% data-management time).
  • Product-specific model generation and lifelong learning (−75% model training/validation time).
  • Open-set model updates and edge deployment/monitoring (−50% engineer intervention).

TV Exterior Inspection System

Jul 2022 – Nov 2022

Deep-learning exterior inspection for 55–85-inch TVs to reduce cosmetic defects (22.6% of field defects).

  • Gap/step inspection system installed and verified on a pilot production line (about 6 s per product).
  • Rear-cover inspection — high-resolution tiling, candidate detection, and false-call removal (about 3.7 s per product).
